Gunja

Botanical Name: Abrus precatorius (L)

Common Name: Gunja / Rosary Pea

Family: Fabaceae

Description

Gunja is a climbing plant with small leaves and distinctive red-and-black seeds.

Medicinal Uses

  • Traditionally used in some systems of medicine for various ailments.
  • Traditionally reported for skin problems and wounds.
  • Traditionally used for fever, cough and cold.
Safety Note: Gunja seeds and other parts of the plant can be highly toxic. They should not be consumed or used without qualified professional supervision.
